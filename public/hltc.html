Senate Health & Long-Term Care Committee

Committees hold public hearings on bills, vote in executive sessions, and study issues in work sessions. Learn about the Senate Health & Long-Term Care Committee (HLTC) and its membership, meetings, and how to participate.

    The Senate Health & Long-Term Care Committee considers issues relating to:

    • Health care services
    • Health care consumer protection
    • Long-term care
    • Behavioral health
    • Licensing and regulation of health care facilities
    • Licensing and regulation of health care professions
    • Health care profession creation and scope of practice
    • Pharmacies and pharmaceutical drugs
    • Financing of healthcare
    • Private health insurance
    • Public employee health insurance
    • Substance abuse and substance use disorder
    • Medicaid
    • Public health issues
